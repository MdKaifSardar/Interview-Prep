# TypeScript Interview Preparation — Final List

This is the **final version I would use for your preparation**. It keeps the core TypeScript system, adds the practical gaps relevant to **Full-Stack / Node / GenAI**, and avoids duplicating your separate JavaScript and React lists.

**Priority**

* **P0** — Must know
* **P1** — Important second-pass material
* **P2** — Advanced / worst-case preparation

---

## 1. TypeScript Fundamentals

1. **P0** — What is TypeScript, and what problems does it solve compared with JavaScript?
2. **P0** — What is the difference between compile-time type checking and runtime validation? Why can't TypeScript itself validate runtime data?
3. **P0** — What is type inference, and when does TypeScript infer a type versus requiring an annotation?
4. **P0** — What is structural typing, and how does it differ from nominal typing?
5. **P1** — What is excess property checking, and why can an object literal fail assignment while an intermediate variable succeeds?

---

## 2. Types, Interfaces, and Object Shapes

6. **P0** — What is the difference between `type` and `interface`? When would you use each?
7. **P0** — What are union types, intersection types, and literal types?
8. **P0** — What are optional properties and `readonly` properties? What does `readonly` actually guarantee?
9. **P0** — What are tuples, and how are they different from arrays?
10. **P1** — What is declaration merging, and why is it one reason to use `interface`?
11. **P1** — What are index signatures such as `[key: string]: T`, and when are they useful?

---

## 3. Type Safety and Narrowing

12. **P0** — What are `any`, `unknown`, and `never`? What are their practical differences?
13. **P0** — What is type narrowing, and how does TypeScript use control-flow analysis and runtime checks to narrow types?
14. **P0** — What is a user-defined type guard? Explain `value is SomeType`.
15. **P0** — What are discriminated unions, and why are they useful?
16. **P0** — What is the difference between `null` and `undefined`, and what does `strictNullChecks` change?
17. **P1** — What is `void`, and how is it different from `undefined` and `never`?

---

## 4. Generics

18. **P0** — What are generics, and why are they preferable to `any` in many situations?
19. **P0** — What does `T extends U` mean in a generic constraint?
20. **P0** — How does generic type inference work?
21. **P0** — How would you design `getProperty<T, K extends keyof T>(obj, key)` while preserving the `T[K]` relationship?
22. **P1** — What are generic default type parameters, and when are they useful?

---

## 5. Type Operators and Assertions

23. **P0** — What is `keyof`, and how is it used with generics?
24. **P0** — What are indexed access types such as `T[K]`?
25. **P0** — What is `typeof` when used in a type position?
26. **P0** — What is a type assertion (`as`)? Does it perform runtime conversion or validation?
27. **P1** — What is `as const`, and how does it affect literal types and readonly behavior?
28. **P1** — What is the `satisfies` operator, and how is it different from a type annotation and `as`?

---

## 6. Utility Types

29. **P0** — What are `Partial`, `Required`, `Readonly`, `Pick`, and `Omit`?
30. **P0** — What are `Record`, `Exclude`, `Extract`, and `NonNullable`?
31. **P0** — What are `ReturnType`, `Parameters`, and `Awaited` used for?

---

## 7. Runtime Data, APIs, and GenAI

32. **P0** — Why doesn't a TypeScript interface or type validate an API response or LLM response at runtime?
33. **P1** — How does a runtime schema validator such as Zod complement TypeScript?
34. **P1** — How do you derive a TypeScript type from a Zod schema using `z.infer`?
35. **P1** — How would you type structured LLM output so that the runtime validation schema and TypeScript type remain consistent?
36. **P1** — How would you model arbitrary or recursive JSON data in TypeScript?
37. **P1** — How do you type asynchronous streams using `AsyncIterable`, `AsyncIterator`, or `AsyncGenerator`?

---

## 8. TypeScript Configuration and Modules

38. **P0** — What does `strict` in `tsconfig.json` mean, and what important checks does it enable?
39. **P1** — What are the important `tsconfig.json` options for a Node/Next.js project, particularly `module`, `moduleResolution`, `esModuleInterop`, `paths`, and `skipLibCheck`?
40. **P1** — What are declaration files (`.d.ts`), and why are they needed?
41. **P1** — What is module augmentation, and how would you use it to extend types from a third-party library such as `Express.Request`?
42. **P1** — What are TypeScript enums, how do they behave at runtime, and what are alternatives such as objects with `as const`?

---

## 9. Advanced TypeScript

43. **P1** — What are conditional types? Explain the `T extends U ? X : Y` pattern.
44. **P1** — What is `infer` in conditional types? Give a practical example.
45. **P1** — What are mapped types, and how can you create your own utility type with them?
46. **P1** — What is `noUncheckedIndexedAccess`, and how does it affect expressions such as `arr[i]`?
47. **P2** — What are distributive conditional types, and when can they produce surprising results?
48. **P2** — What are template literal types, and what practical problems can they solve?
49. **P2** — What are abstract classes and `implements` in TypeScript, and how do they differ from `extends`?
50. **P2** — What are the practical implications of TypeScript's variance and function parameter assignability?
